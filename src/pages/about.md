## Why this site exists

It started as a personal knowledge base — the notes, cheat sheets, and
worked explanations I build for myself while learning a tool or concept. The
guiding rule is *prove it by building it*: whatever gets learned becomes a
small project, a reference guide, or a write-up.

It is published openly because well-organized, plainly-explained reference
material is genuinely hard to find, and because writing for an audience forces
the explanations to actually hold up.

## How the content is created and reviewed

- **Original, not scraped.** Every article, guide, and cheat sheet is written
  from my own understanding and hands-on use. Where a topic draws on external
  documentation or other people's ideas, the piece is my own explanation,
  structure, and examples — not a copy or a reworded version of the source.
- **Sourced.** Content that builds on external material links to the original
  documentation and articles under a *Sources & Further Reading* section so you
  can go to the primary source.
- **Kept current.** Guides are revised as tools change or as I find better ways
  to explain something. Corrections are welcome — see [Contact](/contact).
- **Opinion is labelled as opinion.** Where a piece makes a judgement call
  (tool choice, trade-offs, "what I'd do"), it is presented as my view, not as
  settled fact.
