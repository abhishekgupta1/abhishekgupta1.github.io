# Knowledge Base

Welcome to the Abhishek Gupta Knowledge Base — a curated collection of
technical guides, runbooks, and deep-dives drawn from hands-on experience
across **Test Automation (SDET)**, **Site Reliability Engineering (SRE)**,
**Software Development (SDE)**, **Leadership & Management (MBA)**, and
**AI-driven tooling**.

## Categories

- **SDET Skills** — Test automation, quality engineering, and SDET practices (e.g. Playwright)
- **SRE Skills** — Observability, incident response, and reliability practices (e.g. OpenTelemetry)
- **SDE Skills** — Software development engineering topics and patterns (e.g. Python)
- **MBA Skills** — Business, strategy, and management fundamentals (e.g. leadership scenarios for SDET managers)
- **AI Skills** — AI/ML concepts, tools, and applications (e.g. Claude)

Browse the sidebar to explore each category.
