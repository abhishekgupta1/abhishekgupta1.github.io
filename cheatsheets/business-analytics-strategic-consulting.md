#### Consulting frameworks at a glance

| Framework | Use for |
|---|---|
| SWOT | quick internal-vs-external snapshot |
| PESTLE | external macro forces (political, economic, legal…) |
| Value Chain | where cost/friction actually sits |
| 5 Whys | tracing a symptom to its root cause |

Each answers a different question — using the wrong one gives a tidy but
unhelpful analysis.

</div>

<div class="cheat-card">

#### Financial & legal modeling for consultants

Keep 3-6 months of expenses in reserve — consulting income is lumpy and
client payment terms (often net-30/net-60) mean revenue lags work by months.

</div>

<div class="cheat-card">

#### Value-based pricing & client acquisition

Price against the value the client gets, not hours spent. A two-week
engagement that saves a client $500K is worth far more than hourly billing
would capture — and hourly pricing anchors expectations on your time.

</div>

<div class="cheat-card">

#### Data visualization & storytelling

One chart, one message. If a chart needs a paragraph to explain what it's
showing, simplify the chart — not the audience.

</div>

<div class="cheat-card">

#### Enterprise architecture evaluation

Evaluate engineering operations across four lenses: deployment practices,
testing/QA maturity, architecture coherence, and operational resilience.

</div>

<div class="cheat-card">

#### Predictive vs. prescriptive analytics

Predictive answers "what's likely to happen" (regression, forecasting).
Prescriptive answers "what should we do about it" (optimization, scenario
modeling under constraints).

</div>
